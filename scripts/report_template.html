Where the masks go wrong

The worst cases found in the 150-scan audit, grouped by cause. Before/after pairs show the commit-9bce6ec output next to the current one. The current pipeline routes every case shown here to review except the three fixed primer placements and the Weller false-shear case.

  • Primer mislocalised (fixed). On three E3 full heads the strongest edge circle sat on the headstamp. Candidates are now re-ranked by a primer-diameter prior, concentricity with the case head and a crater inside.
  • False polarity flip (flagged, still wrong). Two E3 primers carry a tall flow-back plateau, which looked like a cast. They are no longer flipped and are flagged, but the crater on the plateau is not separated from the primer.
  • Unmeasured crater (flagged). Confocal scans trimmed to the primer leave the steep crater as a data gap, so its outline and depth are uncertain.
  • Rectangular aperture (improved, flagged). The Glock slot is now detected on 29 of 39 scans and replaces the circular aperture, so the breech face excludes it and the impression stays inside it. The remaining 10 keep the circular model, and every Glock scan is still flagged.
  • False aperture shear (not flagged). When a shallow drag channel is not detected, its walls can pass as shear (Weller TW04-01).

How each mask is made

Primer

Signed circular Hough transform on height gradients within 1.5–3.0 mm radius, refined by 360 rays and a robust circle fit. Candidates are re-ranked by a 3.7–5.6 mm diameter prior, concentricity with the case head and a crater inside.

Datum

Robust plane on the primer face gives depth below the surface. Heights are flipped only when a central bump, stronger primer-edge evidence and a plausible flipped crater all agree.

Firing-pin impression

Hysteresis on depth, grown to 15 % of crater depth; unmeasured holes inside the primer count as crater.

Aperture

A rectangular slot when two long, straight, parallel steps flank the pin (Glock); otherwise the flow-back ring around the crater, whose outer fall-off on the radial median profile gives the hole radius.

Drag

Impression outside the fitted strike circle, kept only if it is elongated and points one way.

Aperture shear

Striations at the aperture edge that run along the drag axis (or radially without drag) and are rougher and more coherent than the breech face.

Breech face

Primer face minus the aperture zone, the rounded cup rim and missing data.

Quality control

A score from primer-edge support, data coverage, crater and aperture evidence, plus hard gates (breech face under 20 % or impression over 50 % of the primer face, implausible primer size, declined flip, unmeasured crater, non-circular impression), flags scans for an examiner with a reason code.
